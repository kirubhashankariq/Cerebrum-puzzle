Curator Guidelines

Mechanical Stewardship Protocols

Keep high-precision alloy and wooden burrs operating smoothly for generations.

Rule 01: Zero Force Policy

All our mechanisms are cut to 0.02mm tolerance. If a piece does not slide freely, you have not reached the proper topological keyway. Never use pliers or force.

Rule 02: Dry PTFE Lube Only

Never apply petroleum oils (WD-40) to brass or wooden mechanisms. Use only micro-fine PTFE dry powder or Renaissance micro-crystalline wax on metal surfaces.

Rule 03: The True Solution

A puzzle is only solved when you can completely reverse every step, restoring the mechanism back to its factory-assembled closed state without reference to diagrams.
