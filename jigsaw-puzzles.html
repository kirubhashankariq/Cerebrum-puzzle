The Anatomy of an Heirloom Jigsaw

Why our wooden and museum-board puzzles feel fundamentally different in your hands.

01

0.05mm Laser Kerf

Industrial CO2 laser optics slice each interlock with near-zero material burn. Adjacent pieces fit so tightly the completed puzzle can be lifted whole from the table.

Zero False Fits
02

Thematic Whimsy Pieces

Interspersed throughout every puzzle are hand-drawn silhouette pieces—astronomical constellations, forest creatures, or artisan tools relevant to the artwork.
