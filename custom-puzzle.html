Clarity & Protocol

Atelier Custom Order FAQ

Everything you need to know about preparing your image and delivery schedules.

What image resolution is required for optimal laser sharpness?

We recommend minimum dimensions of 3000 × 2000 pixels (at least 300 DPI at physical output size). Our prepress technicians inspect every uploaded file and contact you immediately if upscaling is necessary.

How long does custom fabrication take?

100 and 500 piece puzzles require 3-4 business days. 1000 and 2000 piece orders require 5-7 business days to allow archival ink mounting to fully cure before precision CNC laser cutting.

What if a piece is lost during family assembly?

Because every custom puzzle cut path is archived in our CAD database with your order ID, we can re-laser cut and dispatch the exact coordinates of any missing piece within 48 hours.
