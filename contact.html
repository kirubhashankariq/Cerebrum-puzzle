Solver Support

Stuck On a Mechanism? Need Advice?

Never smash a puzzle in frustration. Here is how our solver assistance network operates.

Our hint vault utilizes a five-tier cryptic clue ladder. Clue 1 only hints at the primary axis of rotation; Clue 2 identifies internal decoy cams; only Clue 5 reveals the full disassembly vector. Email hints@cerebrumpuzzles.com with your item number to receive Clue 1.
Submit a request using the form above under "Missing Part or Pin Replacement". Include your shipping address and the puzzle serial number. We maintain an archive of all past precision pins and dispatch replacement hardware free of charge.
All puzzles arrive fully assembled in their locked state inside their presentation chests. Your initial challenge is disassembly; your second (and often vastly harder) challenge is reconstructing the puzzle back into its monolithic locked configuration.
