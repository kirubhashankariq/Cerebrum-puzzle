Our Engineering Axioms

Four non-negotiable principles that dictate every burr, lock, and jigsaw crafted in our atelier.

01

Gravitational Interlock

We prohibit adhesives, screws, or cosmetic fasteners. Puzzles stay assembled purely through internal friction, balanced counterweights, and gravitational equilibrium.

Zero Adhesives Ever
02

Aerospace Metallurgy

Constructed exclusively from naval brass, aircraft-grade 6061-T6 anodized aluminum, and dense phosphor bronze for unmistakable weight and thermal conduction.

Solid Billet Stock
03

Mensa Calibration

Every design is rigorously benchmarked by competition speed solvers and Mensa fellows, certifying true difficulty ratings from Level 1 introductory to Level 10 master.

Cognitive Validation
04

Generational Longevity

Engineered as heirloom pieces meant to be passed down through generations. Resistant to warpage, humidity shifts, and repeated solves over decades.
